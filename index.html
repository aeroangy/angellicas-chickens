The Ladies

Meet the Flock

Four hens, four personalities, four beautiful egg colors. Each one a character in her own right.

🐔 Lap Hen
Mira
Chocolate Mottled Orpington
🤗 The Sweetheart

Mira is our most affectionate hen — true to the Orpington's well-earned reputation for gentle temperament. Given any opportunity, she'll try to roost right in your lap. Raised from a day-old chick, she's been a people-lover from the very start.

Lays large brown eggs, sometimes with speckles
🐓 Top Hen
Dottie
Speckled Sussex
👑 The Boss

Dottie runs a tight ship. As the reigning queen of the pecking order, she's always on a mission — keeping the other hens in line and the flock moving with purpose. Don't let her bossiness fool you; she earned every bit of that rank.

Lays light, creamy-tan eggs
🐦 Free Spirit
Luna
Light Splash Fibrocana
🌙 The Adventurer

Luna is our flighty, boundary-pushing girl. She's always the first to test the edges of the yard and the last to settle — the flock's unofficial sentinel, always scanning, always alert. She came to us from a thoughtful breeding program in Oregon.

Lays soft, ethereal light blue eggs
🐣 Broody Girl
Snow
Light Splash Olive/Moss Egger
🌿 The Dreamer

Last in the pecking order but certainly not least — Snow has one singular dream every spring: becoming a mother. She'll devotedly sit on unfertilized eggs for months at a time. One day, Snow. One day. Her deep teal eggs are the crown jewel of every carton.

Lays rich, jewel-toned deep teal eggs
The Eggs

Which Egg is Which?

One of the greatest joys of a mixed flock is the rainbow in the egg basket. Here's how to tell the ladies apart.

Mira's Eggs
Large and warm brown — the biggest in the basket, sometimes decorated with natural speckles.
Dottie's Eggs
Light, creamy tan — classic and tidy, as orderly and dependable as Dottie herself.
Luna's Eggs
A soft, airy light blue — pale and beautiful, fitting for our most free-spirited hen.
Snow's Eggs
A deep, jewel-toned teal — the most striking egg in the carton, from our most determined girl.
The Backstory

How It All Started

A spring morning, a long line, and a chicken shortage.

Mira and Dottie came home in the spring of 2025, just one day old — tiny, peeping fluffballs from Reber Ranch, just down the road in our area. Getting them wasn't easy: the avian flu was spreading across the country, chicks were scarce, and we stood in line at store opening to secure our spot.

They were raised alongside their brother, Sweet Pea — handled daily, hand-fed, and thoroughly spoiled into the affectionate hens they are today. Mira never forgot those early days of human contact.

Luna & Snow join the family.

Luna and Snow arrived from Red Dog Ranch in Oregon at 7–10 months of age, already full-grown and full of character. Part of a thoughtful Americauna-mixed breeding program designed to produce beautiful birds with striking egg colors, they brought their own dynamic to the yard. The flock found its rhythm — and its pecking order — in time.

🐓 A note about Sweet Pea

Every flock has its stories, and ours includes a handsome rooster named Sweet Pea who joined us from Graham, WA. He was charming, beautiful, and completely devoted to his girls — but he was also exceptionally loud. Our neighbors appreciated us considerably more after we found Sweet Pea a proper farm home with plenty of hens to keep him busy. We miss him. A little.
